01What is Petzo?

Petzo is a digital platform for pet owners. Track your pets’ health data, book vet appointments and much more — all in one place.

02Is the app free?

The core features of Petzo are completely free. A premium plan adds advanced features for owners who want more.

03What is the digital pet passport?

It’s the central place for everything about your pet — vaccinations, exams, therapies and other important health information, ready to share when you need it.

04How do I book a vet appointment?

In the app, choose a clinic from our network, check the available slots and book in just a few taps. You’ll get a reminder before the visit.

05Can I use the app for more than one pet?

Yes. Create a profile for every pet you have and keep their complete records in one place — switching between them takes a single tap.

06What if my vet isn’t on Petzo yet?

Your vet can help you create a profile and activate their clinic through Petzo VMS. You can also suggest a clinic to us and our team will do its best to bring them into the network.

07Can my clinic use Petzo VMS?

Yes. Petzo VMS is built for veterinary practices, with support for multiple users and locations. Book a demo and our team will walk you through it.

08Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Your privacy and data security are our priority, and we use advanced protection methods to keep all data safe.

09How do I contact support?

Email us or use “Contact support” inside the app. For technical issues, leave your phone number in the contact form and the Petzo support team will call you back as soon as possible.
